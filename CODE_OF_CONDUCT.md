# Code of Conduct

ApplySharp follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## TL;DR

Be respectful. Be constructive. Assume good intent.

We welcome contributions from people of every background, skill level, and life situation. Many of us are job seekers ourselves, often under pressure. That context applies to maintainers and contributors alike.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that builds a healthy community:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy toward other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Trolling, insulting comments, or personal attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including the GitHub repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any related public channels.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior via [GitHub's report-content tool](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) or by opening a private [Security Advisory](https://github.com/saitata7/apply-sharp/security/advisories/new). Reports will be reviewed promptly and confidentially.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent consequences as determined by other project leadership.
